# Photo index: which Drive folder each job comes from

Every photo in a job comes from ONE Drive folder and ONE address. Each photo's location tag was checked to be within 150 m of the job's first photo. No coordinates are stored here. Originals are in `raw/drive-all/<Drive folder name>/` and are not in git.

Some photos taken right as someone arrives at a site carry the location of where they just were (kilometers off). Those are never used.
