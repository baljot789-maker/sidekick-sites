## How the home page maps to StoryBrand

| Part | On the page |
|---|---|
| The customer wants something | Hero: a remodel in their region, with the plan and labor cost first |
| External problem | A dated bathroom or kitchen, a storm-damaged roof |
| Internal problem | Worry about a price that keeps climbing, or a crew that disappears |
| Philosophical problem | "You shouldn't have to hand over your savings and hope it works out." |
| Guide: empathy | "Trusting someone with tens of thousands of dollars and your home is a big step." |
| Guide: authority | 10+ years, own roofing crew, FORTIFIED projects, partner trades |
| Plan | Free estimate, clear plan with labor cost, pay as each stage is done. The About page adds the promises ("How we work"). |
| Direct call to action | "Get a free estimate," on every screen |
| Transitional call to action | "See typical prices" |
| Failure | "You can end up paying twice: once for the job, and again to fix it." |
| Success | The bathroom you show company, the kitchen that works, the roof that holds, the bill that matches the plan |

## Jay should approve before launch

- **Every town on the site.** Listing a town tells people we take jobs there. Jackson, Ridgeland, Flowood and Brandon are new and unconfirmed.
- **"You can end up paying twice"** and the success lines. They describe customers' outcomes, not facts about AKM.

- **Publishing the prices.** The brief lists them, and they match the positioning (open about cost, filter out small jobs). Jay should still say yes to seeing them on a public page.
- "Jay goes over the job with you and lays out a clear plan with the labor cost." The brief doesn't say whether the estimate is an in-person visit.
- "Houses from about $250,000" on the home page.
- The commercial kitchen story, especially "raised the property's value."

Licensed and insured, FORTIFIED certified (as a title), real estate services, lender or program names. See preview-gaps.md.
