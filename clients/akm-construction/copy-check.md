## Checked against Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing"

Clear on all of these: AI vocabulary (crucial, meticulous, showcase, testament, vibrant, and the rest), "serves as"/"boasts"/"features" in place of "is"/"has," negative parallelisms ("not X, but Y" and "Y rather than X"), rule of three, vague attribution, trailing "-ing" clauses, title case, em dashes, and curly quotes. The three numbered steps under "How it works" are a real sequence, not a rhetorical triple.
