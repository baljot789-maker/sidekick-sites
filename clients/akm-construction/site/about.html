About

About AKM

Most homeowners worry a remodel will run over budget or never get finished. AKM gives you the plan and the labor cost up front, and you pay only as each stage is done.

10+years in the trade

AKM Real Estate and Construction is a remodeling and building company based in Belzoni, Mississippi. We work across the Delta, the Jackson area and Oxford.

We take on full bathroom and kitchen remodels. We don't do handyman work or small repairs.

Partner contractors cover plumbing, electrical and concrete, so one call can cover the whole project.

How we work

A clear plan first

Before work starts, you know what we'll do, what's possible and why, and what the labor costs.

Pay as each stage is done

We're paid when each part of the job is finished, never for work you can't see.

Materials at cost

Buy materials yourself and have them delivered, or we pick them up and bill them at what we paid.

Built to last

We'd rather tell you a bigger spend is needed, and why, than build something that won't hold up.
