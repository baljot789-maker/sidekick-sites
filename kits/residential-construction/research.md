## Cities searched that turned up no qualifying independent

Birmingham AL, New Orleans LA, Savannah GA, Mobile AL, Huntsville AL, Lafayette LA, Greenville SC, Chattanooga TN, Hattiesburg MS. Houzz counts in the Deep South rarely pass 100. Southern homeowners leave Google reviews.

## Limits of this research

- 12 independents and 7 chains across 11 cities. The kit agent asks for 15 to 20 across 5 metros. The metro count is met, the business count is close.
- Font detection reads what the browser loaded. Wix and Squarespace sites sometimes hide this. Two results come from source code only and are marked.
- Ratings change. These are a snapshot from one day.
