# Patterns: what winning kitchen and bath sites do

From `research.md` (2026-10-19). Counts use the sites that loaded: 10 independents, 4 chains. Rules marked **Rule** are for the Builder and Copy agents on every residential-construction site.

## What the sites do

1. **The headline names the service and the town.** 4 of 10 independents name both, and 2 more name the service only. So do the chain location pages for Five Star (Memphis) and EZ Baths (Louisiana). Examples: "Home remodeling & renovation services in Baton Rouge, LA" and "Premier bathroom remodeling in Charleston and beyond." Two independents use only their brand name (Commonwealth, TilesMax) and two use a slogan (CER, Limitless).
   **Rule:** H1 says what the business does and where. Style it however you like, but the words stay literal.

2. **The phone number is on the first screen.** 5 of 9 independents that loaded, 2 of 3 chains. The ones without it (Commonwealth, Canter, CER, TilesMax) all push a consultation form instead.
   **Rule:** phone in the header on every page, plus a sticky Call and Text bar on phones. (Already in builder.md.)

3. **"Free" is the most common button word.** "Free estimate," "free consultation" or "free in-home" appears on 5 of 10 independents and all 4 chains.
   **Rule:** ask the owner whether estimates are free. If they are, say so on the main button. Never assume it.

4. **Reviews sit on the homepage.** 6 of 10 independents show testimonials or a review widget on the homepage.
   **Rule:** once the owner approves real reviews, give them a homepage section. Never fill that section with "customers say."

5. **Photos show finished rooms.** The independent sites lead with project photos. I didn't check each one for stock images.
   **Rule:** a remodeler's site goes live only with real job photos. Previews may show placeholders.

## How they look

- **The firms that feel Southern and upscale use classic serifs.** Commonwealth (Charleston) uses Gideon Roman, Canter (Charleston) uses Orpheus Pro, and CER (Atlanta) uses Cormorant. All three set them at regular or light weights. None used a heavy display serif.
- **The volume firms use bold geometric sans.** Open Sans 800, Montserrat, Poppins, Mulish, DM Sans. Three of those five are on our banned list.
- **No site used DM Serif Display.** It's a common pick for AI-built sites, and the first AKM draft used it. That draft has been replaced.

## Copy

- Real local copy is plain and literal. For example, Lacoste & Young writes "Over ten years of experience in renovation." They earn their ratings with the work and the reviews, not the writing.
- The AI-sounding lines on these sites are slogans. Examples: "Elevate your home with affordable luxury" (West Shore), "Spaces crafted to be lived in, not just admired" (CER, a negative parallelism), and "Transforming homes into dream homes" (Commonwealth).
  **Rule:** write like the small independents. Plain verbs, "is" and "are," no slogans.
